Cards are surface/raised on a surface/base canvas; the dot grid drops to #232A35 at 1.31:1, deliberately below the boundary threshold because it is texture, not structure. Connector lines use border/strong at 4.79:1 — they are structure.

Reparent — pointer and keyboard

Dragging a card lifts it to elevation/3, dims its former connector to 40%, and outlines every legal drop target with a 2px dashed accent ring. Illegal targets — the node itself, its own descendants, and anyone outside the programme — get no ring and reject the drop with a shake of 4px over 120ms (suppressed under reduced motion, replaced by a status/danger border flash).
The keyboard path is identical in outcome and announced at each step: R enters reparent mode with the current manager preselected, ↑↓ walks the legal-target list in tree order, Enter commits, Escape restores. No pointer-only capability exists in this surface.
