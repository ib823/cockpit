Loading identity

The beacon emits. A wave leaves the dot and travels out past the inner arc to the ring — the same gesture the mark already draws, now in time. No glow, no gradient, no flare: only the mark’s own two colours, opacity and scale.

Indeterminate — light
For waits of unknown length. The structure sits at 28% as its own track, so the mark is legible at every frame and never disassembles.
Indeterminate — dark
Dark keeps the gold unchanged at 11.09:1 on surface/base and lifts the strokes to content/primary. The dot is the one element that never changes colour between themes.
Determinate
The outer ring is a 88.3-unit track that fills clockwise from the bottom gap. Shown at 64% — the splash on / knows how many queued changes it is reading, so it must not pretend otherwise.
Reduced motion
Nothing scales and nothing pulses. The ring advances in discrete steps of 12.5% and the word beneath it changes — progress is carried by the text, which is where it belonged anyway. Applies to indeterminate waits too: the arc steps rather than sweeps.

Placement — every surface, desktop and mobile

Two frames first, at real size, then the exhaustive table. The governing rule: the mark identifies the product, it does not decorate the screen — inside the workspace it appears exactly once, in the top-left, and nowhere else.

App icon — the one place the mark sits on a field
Slate field, light strokes, gold dot. A white-background icon disappears into a light home screen and reads as a missing asset. Mark occupies 62% of the canvas; the safe area is the remaining 19% per side. Android’s monochrome notification icon is the third form — one colour, dot filled solid, no gold, because the OS will discard it anyway.
