The chip is a button whose accessible name is the whole state sentence; activating it opens the sync popover with pending changes, last successful sync, queue size and Retry. It appears in the top bar of every authenticated screen at every breakpoint — at 320px it keeps the glyph and one word and drops the timestamp.
